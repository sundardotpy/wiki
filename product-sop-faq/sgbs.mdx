## Dashboard tracking discontinued from 15th December

Users have historically been able to view and track their **Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB)** investments on the Wint Wealth dashboard, even though the units themselves were never held with Wint Wealth. Since the units are not available with us, we cannot guarantee accurate tracking of them going forward.

<Warning>
  **SGB tracking on the Wint Wealth dashboard is discontinued from 15th December.** This is a display/tracking change only — it does not affect the user's actual SGB investment in any way.
</Warning>

### What this means for the user

* All SGB units continue to remain **safely held with the platform originally used to purchase them — Zerodha**.
* The user will **no longer see their SGB units on the Wint Wealth dashboard** after 15th December.
* This change does **not** impact the SGB investment, holdings, maturity, or interest in any way.

### Where to track SGB going forward

Users can continue tracking their SGB holdings directly on **Zerodha**, the same platform they used at the time of purchase.

### What we can still help with

Even though SGB tracking is no longer shown on the dashboard, support can still assist with:

* Any information the user needs about their **past SGB investments**
* Any **documents or clarifications** they want from our side

## Handling user queries

**Response to user:**

> We're writing to inform you about an upcoming change related to your Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) investments through Wint Wealth. Over the past few years, you've been able to view and track your SGB investments on your Wint Wealth dashboard. As the units are not available with us, we are not able to share the accurate tracking of it. Hence the tracking of SGB investments will be discontinued from 15th December.
>
> Your SGB units continue to remain safely held with Zerodha, the platform you originally used to purchase them. You will no longer see your SGB units on the Wint Wealth dashboard after 15th December, but this does not affect your SGB investment, holdings, maturity, or interest in any way. You can continue tracking your holdings directly on Zerodha, just like you did at the time of purchase.
>
> While SGB tracking will no longer be available on Wint Wealth, our team is fully available to help you with any information about your past SGB investments or any documents/clarifications you need — just reply and we'll take care of it.
